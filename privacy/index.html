What data we use and where it comes from

When we prepare an example website for a business, we use information the business has made public:

  • business name, address, phone number, email address and opening hours;
  • photos, logo, menu or price information and reviews published by or about the business;

Sources: the business's Google Maps listing, its own website, and its public Facebook and Instagram pages. We do not buy contact lists.

If you reply to us or become a customer, we also process the content of our correspondence and the data needed for an offer, an invoice and the website itself.

Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • Contacting businesses and building an example website. Our legitimate interest in offering our service to businesses (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Direct marketing can be a legitimate interest (Recital 47 GDPR).
  • Answering your messages, preparing offers and delivering a website. Steps before or for a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Invoices and accounting. Our legal obligations under tax law (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Counting how often example websites are opened. Our legitimate interest in knowing whether an example was looked at, without identifying anyone (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). See "Example websites" below.
  • Keeping a "do not contact" list. Our legitimate interest in respecting objections and not contacting you again (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If you want an offer or a website from us, we need your business details for the offer and the invoice; without them we cannot enter into a contract. You do not have to give us any data just because we contacted you.

Your right to object

You can object at any time to any processing based on our legitimate interests (Article 21(1) GDPR), and in particular to us contacting you for marketing purposes (Article 21(2) GDPR).

Just reply "stop" to any of our messages, by email, Instagram, Messenger or WhatsApp, or write to studio@tabelasites.com. We will not contact you again and will delete the example website. We keep only the contact detail we used (for example your email address, Instagram name or phone number) on a "do not contact" list, so that we can respect your objection.

Example websites

Example websites are hosted on GitHub Pages under vorschau.tabelasites.com. We send the link only to the business the example was made for; the pages are not linked from anywhere else and ask search engines not to list them. An example is deleted if the business is not interested.

Anonymous visit count. Example websites use an anonymous counter that runs via klickmill.app, our own counting server. It sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser (neither session storage nor local storage). It counts per page view: at the earliest five seconds after the page was opened, and only after you have scrolled or tapped (a mouse click or key press also counts), the page sends one request that contains only the name of the example and the event "opened". If you click the WhatsApp button, the page sends one more request of the same kind ("clicked the WhatsApp button"). The server stores only one number per day, example and event. It does not store your IP address, browser details or any identifier. Your browser necessarily sends your IP address with the request so that it can reach the server, but the address is not recorded.

Separately from the counter, your browser may remember the language you chose (local storage); this stays on your device. When you open a page, GitHub, as the host, receives technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver it. Example websites may contain buttons to call or email the business, or to message us on WhatsApp; if you use them, your phone or the respective provider processes the data needed for that.

These providers process data for us or as independent providers of their services. Where data is transferred to the United States, this is based on one of the following: the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (European Commission adequacy decision of 10 July 2023), where the provider is certified under it, or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards at studio@tabelasites.com.

How long we keep data
